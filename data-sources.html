About Data Sources

Last updated May 22, 2026.

Lapit combines built-in starter pins, OpenStreetMap-derived nearby transit discovery, and community-submitted pins/routes. Built-in pins are a starter layer, not a complete official database.

OpenStreetMap data is contributed by the OSM community. Map display uses MapLibre with map tiles and attribution shown on the map. Lapit does not use scraped Google Maps data as built-in pins.

Community data can be incomplete or wrong. The best version of Lapit improves through confirmations, reports, and admin moderation.
