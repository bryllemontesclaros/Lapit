Privacy Policy

Last updated May 22, 2026.

Lapit uses your browser location only when you allow location access. Your location is used in the app to show nearby transit spots and calculate distance. Lapit does not intentionally store your live location in Supabase.

If you create an account, Supabase Auth stores your email and authentication details. Community pins, route connections, confirmations, reports, and moderation data may be stored so everyone can see shared transit information.

The app uses MapLibre, OpenStreetMap-related map data, Esri map tiles, Supabase, and browser APIs. These services may receive normal technical information such as IP address, device/browser details, and requested map areas.

Do not submit private personal information in pin names, notes, reports, or routes. Community submissions are intended to be public.

For privacy questions, contact montesclarosbrylle@gmail.com.
